# Financial client data analysis
Conducted EDA on a bank's client-level financial dataset (deposits, credit, loans, superannuation, risk weighting) to uncover asset-liability and risk patterns.
This repository presents the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of a Banking Dataset containing information about customers and their financial behavior. The dataset includes a range of features that can be analyzed to gain insights into customer demographics, banking products, financial patterns, and risk factors.

# Dataset description
- Client ID: A unique identifier assigned to each client.
- Name: The name of the client.
- Age: The client's age.
- Location ID: A unique identifier representing the client's geographical location.
- Joined Bank: The date on which the client became a customer of the bank.
- Banking Contact: The client's preferred method of communication with the bank.
- Nationality: The nationality of the client.
- Occupation: The client's profession or job role.
- Fee Structure: Details regarding the fees associated with the client's banking accounts or services.
- Loyalty Classification: A category indicating the client's level of loyalty to the bank.
- Estimated Income: The client's estimated annual income.
- Superannuation Savings: The amount accumulated in the client's superannuation or retirement savings account.
- Amount of Credit Cards: The total number of credit cards owned by the client.
- Credit Card Balance: The outstanding balance on the client's credit cards.
- Bank Loans: The total value of personal loans held by the client.
- Bank Deposits: The total amount deposited by the client.
- Checking Accounts: The balance maintained in the client's checking account.
- Saving Accounts: The balance maintained in the client's savings account.
- Foreign Currency Account: The balance held in foreign currency accounts, where applicable.
- Business Lending: The total amount borrowed by the client for business-related purposes.
- Properties Owned: The number of properties owned by the client.
- Risk Weighting: A score representing the client's assessed financial risk.
- BRId: An identifier used to represent the client's banking relationship or linked accounts.
- GenderId: An identifier representing the client's gender.
- IAId: An identifier associated with internal assessment records.
- Income_band: A category representing the client's income range.

# Objectives

The main objectives of conducting Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Banking Dataset are:
1. Analyze Client Demographics
    - Examine the distribution of clients based on age, nationality, and occupation.
    - Identify distinct customer segments and demographic patterns.
2. Evaluate Financial Behavior
    - Investigate the relationships among income, savings, loans, and credit card usage.
    - Determine the total deposits and loan amounts held by clients.
3. Discover Trends and Patterns
    - Analyze correlations between financial products and customer demographics.
    - Examine how loyalty classifications influence the usage of various banking products.
4. Assess Customer Risk
    - Analyze risk weightings based on clients' financial behaviors and their use of different banking products.
    - Identify patterns that may be associated with higher or lower levels of financial risk.
